# strandbeest

A small motorized build of Theo Jansen's Strandbeest walking mechanism. This is the leg linkage he originally found by running an evolutionary simulation to search for a walking gait, using genetic algorithms to optimize the linkage dimensions over many generations. He did all that in 1990 on an old Atari computer and actually found meaningful dimensions that translated to something useful irl.  


https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/7de8e835-be41-425a-ab04-6f6e2ed2f46c


Went down that rabbit hole, then just modelled and 3D printed his published linkage dimensions to stick a motor on it. It was cool to look at the linkage move the intended way, but the weight balancing was horribly off because of the batteries, and I just moved on to something else.  


## Build
3D-printed legs using Jansen's published linkage dimensions  
Driven by a single motor turning the central crank, moving all legs in sync
